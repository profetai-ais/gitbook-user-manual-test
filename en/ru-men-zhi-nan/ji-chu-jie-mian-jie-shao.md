## Display language

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image .png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Click the "Preferences" icon in the column on the right side of the screen.
2. On the Preferences page, click the Supported Languages ​​drop-down menu.
3. In the "Display Language" option, select the interface language to be displayed (for example: English / Traditional Chinese / Simplified Chinese / Japanese / Thai).

> Note: In the Thai interface, Agent and Token are still displayed in English for the time being.
